Chapter 1047: Storm III

Figures, either flying or sprinting, continuously cut through the chaotic city, heading toward Lu Sheng’s location.

One-third of Anming City had already been destroyed by the frantic fighting. The death matches between Reincarnators and the clean-up operations by the Nine Lives Hall cadres had combined to drastically increase the level of destruction across the city.

Regardless, out of a sense of humanitarianism and the awareness that this was their headquarters, the cadres and disciples of the Nine Lives Hall continued to rescue survivors while purging zombies and Reincarnators.

Their "rescue" was less about active care and more about simply eliminating threats. Starting from the Nine Lives Hall headquarters, various cadres led their members as they pushed outward toward every corner of the city. Secured areas were handed over to the remnants of the police force and small military units for coordinated defense.

Some of the rescued wealthy elites, still shaken, waved checks in the air, demanding to hire the Nine Lives Hall to save their families and daughters. But the Nine Lives Hall ignored them entirely.

The crisis had erupted so suddenly that many upper-class individuals were completely unprepared. The virus had only been spreading for an hour, but nearly all of Anming City had succumbed to the infection. According to statistics, a significant number of people seemed immune—many healthy individuals with strong constitutions managed to survive the crisis. The most tragic victims, however, were the children.

Their constitutions were weak, and the virus spread through them the fastest. In this outbreak, almost no child under the age of ten in Anming City had been spared.

Buildings lay in various states of ruin. Most survivors, under the guidance of the Nine Lives Hall, were permitted to enter the defensive lines only after undergoing physical screenings to ensure they had no open wounds. Two hours later, half of the city had been completely purged. Only the worst-affected area remained: the central commercial district, where the zombies were most concentrated and the Reincarnators were most numerous.

***

Lu Sheng stood silently on the roof of a three-story villa. Behind him, a pair of fleshy wings over five meters wide flapped slowly, occasionally kicking up gusts of wind. Below him, figures—both familiar and unfamiliar—appeared amidst the ruins, kneeling on one knee toward him from a distance.

The Extinguishing Emotion Seed did not require Lu Sheng’s active control; it was a passive trigger. Anyone he defeated but did not kill would have their mental defenses forcibly breached, and an Extinguishing Emotion Seed would be planted in the deepest recesses of their consciousness. In theory, only those with exceptionally resilient wills could resist the implantation of the seed. However, to this day, Lu Sheng had yet to encounter anyone with willpower strong enough to achieve that.

The number of people below grew steadily, increasing from single digits to dozens. Soon, two key figures appeared.

Bright, who had previously been locked in combat and had opened the demonic Slaughter Gate, was now trembling. He stared at Lu Sheng with eyes full of terror, yet his feet moved toward Lu Sheng of their own accord. He was terrified, yet he had no choice but to obey Lu Sheng’s unspoken authority.

The other was Bai Songdie, who had previously escaped from Lu Sheng’s grasp. Her arms were covered in blood, seemingly from a recent battle. Her face was pale and pitiful, looking like a wounded cub in desperate need of comfort and protection.

Lu Sheng didn't know that Bai Songdie was the one who had released the virus. He simply swept his gaze over her before looking away. Bai Songdie’s trembling body finally began to settle. Her greatest fear was Lu Sheng discovering her role in the outbreak.

The flaw of the Extinguishing Emotion Seed became apparent here: the fear it generated caused people to revere and obey Lu Sheng, but that same fear could lead them to hide critical information to avoid even more terrifying consequences.

After a short wait, several more people emerged from the surroundings. All of them were Reincarnators. When no one else arrived, Lu Sheng finally spoke.

"Regardless of where you come from or what mission you carry. Now, you are all my servants." He paused, his gaze landing on Bright.

Bang!!!

Before Bright could even speak, he felt a sudden, sharp pain in his forehead. His vision went black, and he knew no more. To the others, it looked as if Bright had simply exploded, turning into a cloud of blood mist that was instantly scattered by the wind. Only a few exquisite black boxes fell to the ground where he had stood.

"The rest of you, go and purge the remaining districts. Remember to wear the special insignia of the Nine Lives Hall," Lu Sheng said flatly.

The crowd below bowed and then sprinted off into the distance in silence. Bai Songdie felt a surge of relief and was about to turn and fly away.

"You stay," Lu Sheng’s voice suddenly rang in her ear.

Bai Songdie shuddered, nearly bolting in panic, but the terror and reverence instilled by the Extinguishing Emotion Seed forced her to stop in her tracks.

"Your heart is beating twelve times faster per minute than the others," Lu Sheng said, his body blurring into a series of afterimages as he appeared behind her in an instant.

"Tell me, who is the strongest among your people in this descent? Was it that old man just now?" Lu Sheng intended to pry some secrets about the Reincarnators from her.

"I don't..." Bai Songdie turned around with a sob on her face.

"Who released the virus?" Lu Sheng asked suddenly.

"I—it wasn't me!! Ahhh!!!" Bai Songdie was suddenly overcome with terror, backing away repeatedly as her emotions spiraled out of control. Seeing this reaction, Lu Sheng already had his answer.

"Is there more? Hand it over," he said calmly.

Panic-stricken, Bai Songdie retreated several steps, her face flickering through hesitation, doubt, and agony. Finally, she reached out her hand. With a flash of light in her palm, two sealed test tubes filled with a swirling silver liquid appeared.

Lu Sheng took the tubes and held them up to the light. "Let's go. Tell me about every key figure you know." He grabbed Bai Songdie by her long hair and dragged her along as he leaped into the air, flying toward the distance.

***

The commercial district was the area with the highest concentration of shopping malls. A dense, black mass of zombies wandered the vicinity, one after another, strolling through the streets just as they had in life. Their skin had rotted, and decaying flesh was visible in many places. Most of their organs, aside from the digestive system, had completely failed.

From high above, the entire area within a several-hundred-meter radius was a sea of countless, densely packed zombies.

Clang.

Suddenly, a crisp, ear-piercing metallic sound echoed from the front of a hardware store. Massive crowds of zombies hissed and roared, lifting their heads and slowly shuffling toward the source of the noise.

Clang! Clang! Clang! Clang!

A series of even more intense metallic strikes followed. The zombies, now certain of the location, accelerated toward the sound. Groups merged into larger hordes, converging on the source.

Ansha stood there, twirling a short blade in his hand, rhythmically striking several metal pipes on the ground in front of the hardware store. He watched the approaching black tide of corpses with a cold, indifferent expression.

"As I thought... no matter how many of these meaningless pieces of trash there are, they’re still just trash."

Soon, the zombies had gathered in sufficient numbers. He stopped striking the pipes and held up his short blade. With the back of the blade facing him, he flicked it with his finger.

Boom!!!

In an instant, the short blade shattered completely, transforming into countless metallic fragments. These fragments exploded again into even finer silver grit, forming a long dragon of silver mist that blew violently into the zombie tide.

Whoosh—

A continuous series of faint pfft-pfft-pfft sounds followed. In an instant, the entire zombie horde stiffened into metallic statues. Ansha turned around and walked away without a backward glance. Behind him, the massive horde of zombies collapsed, disintegrating into piles of shredded meat.

***

At the edge of another large shopping mall, half of the triangular building was slowly collapsing, kicking up clouds of grayish-yellow dust.

Boom!!!

From the intact half of the building, a large section of floor suddenly exploded outward. Amidst the dust, Hongci’s massive, four-meter-tall frame slowly emerged. She leaped lightly to the ground, landing in the middle of the wreckage.

Green, vein-like patterns pulsed across her skin, and green pipes on her face rapidly transported massive quantities of green liquid. Her muscular build had reached a grotesque level, resembling a pile of tumors or the chaotic tangle of tree roots beneath an ancient giant tree. Some of her muscle masses were even larger than her head.

"Come out! You wretched runts!" Hongci walked over the rubble of the ruined building, her roar causing the ground to vibrate.

"Ah!" A few men and women hiding in the crevices of a broken wall were startled out of their cover, fleeing in terror. Hongci didn't even look at them; she was searching for the Reincarnators she had just been fighting. "Reincarnators"—that was what her master, Wang Mu, had called them. She didn't know what it meant, but it didn't matter. They were all dead men walking anyway.

"Mere NPC!!" a sinister voice suddenly rang out. At the same time, a gray figure lunged toward Hongci with incredible speed. Two other gray figures stood further back, emerging from the dust. They held complex, crossbow-like machines, aimed directly at Hongci.

"Thunder Dragon!"

轟!! Two bolts shot from the crossbows, instantly transforming into two long dragons of blue electricity in mid-air.

"Seeking death!" Hongci arched her back. Her palms vibrated rapidly, gathering a vast cloud of dark green light particles.

"Nether Star Soaring!!"

She slammed her palms into the ground. A massive roar of dark green light erupted, diving into the earth. There was a moment of absolute silence. Boom!! The ground exploded. Countless stones shot out in all directions like cannonballs. The Thunder Dragons and the gray figure were instantly struck, sent flying back while coughing up blood.

***

Wei Handong wore a black leather suit, his strong chest exposed. Hanging from his neck was a black jade pendant his sister had given him, shaped like a strange inverted crescent moon. He was dragging a masked woman covered in blood by her leg. Although the woman was quite beautiful, Wei Handong had zero interest in any woman other than his sister.

The moment this woman had tried to use her charms on him, he had countered with a "Black Tiger Steals Heart" strike, ending the fight instantly.

"Xiao Lian!! Ahhhh! I’ll kill you!!" A black figure flashed into existence from behind, jumping out with a heavy battle-axe glowing with white light. He swung it down with all his might, slamming it directly onto Wei Handong’s forehead.

Clang!!!!

A massive sound, like the ringing of a giant bell, exploded. The ground beneath Wei Handong’s feet cracked and splintered, but he remained unmoved, staring indifferently at the man in front of him. This wasn't the first time this had happened. This man possessed a teleportation-like technique. Wei Handong couldn't catch him, but the man couldn't cut through him either. He eventually decided not to bother attacking anymore—he would just let the man keep swinging.
