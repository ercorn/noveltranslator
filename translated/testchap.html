Chapter 1047: Storm III

Sitting in the shop’s lobby, Lu Sheng gazed through the glass storefront at the dead, silent city streets outside.

The city's power grid seemed to be destroyed; the lights wouldn't turn on, and there was no running water. Lu Sheng decided to rest for the night and search the surrounding area the next morning to see if he could find any food. Relying solely on the small amount of food he carried, he could only last two days at most.

There was no conversation, no movement, not even the sound of insects. Lu Sheng sat alone in the darkness, leaning against a long leather sofa in the shop. He lay on his side and slowly entered a state of meditation. It would take at least three days for his body to fully adapt to the tenth stage of the Assassination Fist before he could proceed to the next level of enhancement.

Time flowed slowly. Unconsciously, Lu Sheng finished his meditation and drifted into a light sleep. Although he was sleeping, it was a shallow rest; any hint of a threat from the outside would instantly snap him back to full alertness.

To his surprise, however, he actually dreamed. In the dream, he stood beneath a colossal black sun. Just by lifting his head, he could see the black sun radiating a bone-chilling, piercing cold. The sun seemed to be sinking lower and lower, constantly drawing closer. He felt as though he were about to be frozen into a popsicle.

Whoosh.

Slowly opening his eyes, Lu Sheng woke naturally as a faint white light hit his eyelids. Rolling out of bed, he ate some crackers and drank a bit of water he had brought along. After straightening his clothes, he continued his exploration deeper into Gelford.

He soon found an intact minivan. He hauled several large canisters of gasoline from a gas station onto the vehicle and began a city-wide search for food. His previous encounters had made him realize that trying to establish a stable base before reaching a certain level of strength was almost a fool's errand. If nothing else, that woman in white could easily destroy everything, rendering all his efforts to build a foundation futile.

For two consecutive days, Lu Sheng did not re-enter the Painful Perception. Even for his current self, that place was far too dangerous. Eventually, in a large supermarket in Gelford, Lu Sheng found two entire crates of dry rations and drinking water, along with some nutritional supplements to prevent micronutrient deficiencies.

Driving the black minivan, Lu Sheng followed the map, speeding toward the direction of Asom. As he left Gelford, the number of abandoned vehicles on the highway dwindled, and buildings appeared only occasionally. In their place was a desolate, overgrown plain of weeds.

He drove for an entire day, following the route on the map. Without the internet, his phone and computer were of little use. While scavenging for food, Lu Sheng had installed a solar charging panel on the roof of the van to meet his temporary electricity needs.

By the afternoon of the third day of driving, the sky began to grow dim. A small gas station gradually appeared on the side of the highway ahead. Lu Sheng slowed down and honked the horn as was his custom.

Beep! Beep!

This was a signal to any survivors that someone had arrived. If there were people living here, they would hear the sound and come out on their own. Opening the car door, Lu Sheng put on his gloves and stepped out.

The White Knight's Gloves, after leaving the Painful Perception, appeared as soft white leather gloves made of an unknown material. Wearing them gave him a slight burning sensation, as if they were corrosive, but to Lu Sheng’s strengthened body, it was negligible.

The gas station was bathed in the afterglow of the setting sun, quiet and desolate. A gust of wind blew, sending dry leaves and grass swirling across the ground. Lu Sheng sighed and walked toward the gas station's convenience store.

Chirp.

To his surprise, the power here was still on. The automatic sensor doors slid open, emitting a crisp ding-dong sound. Simultaneously, the dark barrel of a shotgun appeared, aimed straight at Lu Sheng's nose.

"Don't move!" A muscular black man with a ponytail, wearing a twisted smile, held the gun leveled at Lu Sheng.

"Talk about luck! Look at this, another fat sheep! With all this meat on him, our brothers can eat for another week!" The man chuckled.

A tall, sturdy white man in jeans and a black shirt walked out from the back of the store, holding a shotgun and likewise aiming it at Lu Sheng. "Don't be in such a hurry. Ask him if there's anyone else with him!" The white man licked his lips and said in a deep voice.

Lu Sheng glanced at the two of them, a warm smile appearing on his face. "Is it just the two of you here?"

"Maybe. Who knows? There might be a few more in a few days," the white man replied with a cold sneer.

"Then, do you have food and drink here?" Lu Sheng asked again.

"Of course we do. Aren't you it? Hahaha!" Both men couldn't help but burst into laughter.

Lu Sheng scanned the inside of the shop. Rows of shelves were indeed packed with various types of food and drinks. There were six rows of shelves in total, all stuffed to the brim. "Then, can I take some?" Lu Sheng asked with a smile.

"Are you crazy?" The black man clicked the safety off.

Lu Sheng smiled and walked straight toward one of the shelves, completely ignoring the gun barrels inches away from him. "Crazy!! Stop! I told you to f*cking stop!!"

Bang!!

The black man’s face twisted as he pulled the trigger hard. The bullet grazed Lu Sheng, hitting a freezer behind him and punching a black hole through the metal casing. Lu Sheng, meanwhile, had already found a plastic bag and began stuffing it with large handfuls of food.

"To hell with you!" The white man couldn't take it anymore either and began firing wildly at Lu Sheng.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

A dense hail of bullets fell continuously around Lu Sheng, but strangely, not a single one hit him. This was the terrifying power of the Ten Rings of Mind Delusion. From the very first moment they saw Lu Sheng, these two had already fallen under its spell. This special ability, which carried hypnotic properties, not only caused visual displacement but also induced auditory hallucinations.

Bam!!

Suddenly, the gunfire stopped. The white man had somehow fired a quick, accurate shot into the black man's forehead. Brain matter and blood splattered everywhere. The black man’s expression went blank, his face riddled with a bloody hole as he collapsed backward, his gun clattering to the floor.

Lu Sheng finished packing the food and drinks, stood up, and slowly walked out of the store. Behind him, the white man was frantically waving his shotgun and firing at random. Finally, he turned the barrel toward his own cheek.

"You dare steal my food? Die!!"

Bang.

Everything went quiet.

Lu Sheng walked out of the gas station, and just as he was about to reach his van, he suddenly heard faint cries for help. He paused, his keen senses quickly pinpointing the source. "It's underground."

He turned back around and re-entered the store. Ignoring the two corpses on the floor, he walked into a back room. Inside the storage room filled with crates of goods, there was a wooden cellar door in the corner. Lu Sheng walked over, knelt down, and unlatched the lid. A flight of stone steps extended downward into the dark.

"Help!!"
"Save me!!"
"I beg of you!!"
"Someone, help!!"

A chorus of voices drifted up from below. Lu Sheng stepped down into a spacious basement. The rectangular room was set up like a prison, filled with many large black iron cages. Most of the cages held one or two living people—men, women, elderly, and children, though most were young. The moment Lu Sheng appeared, the group became hysterical, screaming frantically.

Looking at the dozen or so survivors, a gentle smile appeared on Lu Sheng's face. "Does anyone know how to get to Asom?" A faint, white-speckled whirlpool flickered in his eyes.

The noise died down instantly. Everyone’s emotions were suppressed by a mysterious pressure, and they gradually grew too afraid to speak.

"Does no one know?" Lu Sheng asked, sounding disappointed.

"There's no need to go to Asom!" an old man in one of the cages shouted. "This is an emergency shelter I designed myself. It can hold up to fifty people."

"I came from Asom," a young woman shrieked. "Only the Two-Fold People can enjoy anything there. For ordinary people, other than staying alive, you're treated like a dog! I couldn't stand it, so I escaped."

"It was those two psychos, Victor and his partner, who drugged us!"

"All the large settlements are the same. I came from Belga, and it was the same there. Two-Fold People can commit murder without punishment! It’s insane!"

Many of the others began to decry the exploitation of ordinary people in other settlements. Lu Sheng also learned from their words that this place was originally a small settlement. The leader was the old man who had spoken first. His name was Buck, seventy-nine years old, and a former university professor of physics and engineering. He had led the group in building this temporary shelter.

He hadn't expected the black and white men they had taken in to drug everyone and throw them into cages. "So, if I let you out, what's in it for me?" Lu Sheng was not a villain, but he wasn't a saint either. He was simply a young man with a bottom line. He didn't go out of his way to do evil, but he didn't do good for free. He could release them, but he had to get enough benefits first.

The people in the cages went quiet, all eyes turning to old man Buck. The old man rubbed his bald head and gave a bitter smile.

"We can nominate you as our leader. It’s clear you dealt with those two bastards easily. You should know that one of them served in the Marines. For you to handle them so effortlessly shows your strength is immense."

Lu Sheng thought about it. "Does anyone here practice the Phantom Heart Style?"

The status of the Phantom Heart Style here was like Taekwondo, Jiu-Jitsu, or Boxing on Earth—very popular. When he asked this, a man and a woman quickly stood up. "I’ve studied it!" "I’m a Phantom Heart Style instructor!"

Lu Sheng smiled. "Actually, I am a Phantom Heart Style combat master who has spent many years in seclusion in the deep mountains. Now that the world has turned dark and lives are being lost, it is the perfect time for martial artists like us to re-establish order and restore the glory of the Martial Dao!"

The two of them looked at him with complete bewilderment. To them, martial arts was just a hobby. It seemed the man in front of them had some mental issues, but they decided they had better play along for now.
